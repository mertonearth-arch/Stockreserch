How this list runs itself

Two passes a day

A light pre-market pass checks the movers list and any overnight news or filing on names already tracked. A full pass after the close re-researches everything that came due and rebuilds this page.

Cadence, not a ranking

Every new name starts daily. Three straight full passes with nothing new drops it to weekly; three quiet weeks drops it to monthly; three quiet months archives it. Tiers are about attention, not quality.

What counts as "something new"

A dated catalyst, a change in EPS, P/E, revenue, guidance, share count or earnings date, a material filing, an 8%+ session move, or RVOL at 3x or better. Earnings inside 14 days pin a name to daily on their own.

Gaps stay gaps

A figure that could not be established reads not disclosed rather than being inferred, and a negative-earnings company shows no P/E instead of a negative multiple. Where sources disagree, both readings are shown on the name.

Research notes, not advice. Every figure here comes from a public source at the time of the pass and can be stale, revised, or wrong; earnings dates in particular move. Nothing on this page is a recommendation, a rating, or a price target — there is deliberately no score anywhere in it. Verify anything you would act on.

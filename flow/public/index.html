How to read this

Each platform is a sector, sampled by six of its largest stocks. Tower height is dollar volume traded so far in the session. Tower colour is net flow as a share of that volume.

Net flow is a proxy: a 5-minute bar's dollar volume counts as inflow if the typical price rose from the prior bar and outflow if it fell. Every trade has a buyer and a seller, so this shows which side was moving price, not cash entering or leaving the market.

Particles show the last 30 minutes: toward a sector for net inflow, back to the core for net outflow. Density follows the size of the flow.
